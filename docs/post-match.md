# Post-Match

Source: GM2 V3.0, section 3.4.3 and rules <G04> and <G06>.

## End Conditions

The match ends when the 3 minute timer expires or the technician decides to stop it early.

---

## Technician

1. Stop all robot units without disturbing game element positions.
2. Wait for the referee signal.
3. Retrieve the robot.

Gameplay after the timer ends, or after the technician begins to stop the robot, does not count.

A delay of the match from a very complicated retrieval gives a **Yellow Card**.

---

## Auto-Shutoff

GM2 highly suggests a mechanism that stops the robot automatically 3 minutes after the race starts. Manual stopping is allowed. It can cause scoring problems if the robot is interacting with a game element when time expires.

---

## Referee Steps

1. Finalize the score.
2. Signal the technician to retrieve the robot.
3. Field personnel reset the field for the next match.

---

## Emergency Stop

The robot must be stoppable with its emergency stop button for safe retrieval (GM2 <G04>, GM1 <R04>).
