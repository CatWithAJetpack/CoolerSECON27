# Scoring

Source: GM2 V3.0, section 3.6 and Appendix G.

## Points Table

| Achievement | Points |
|---|---|
| Robot enters the course | 25 |
| Cross the finish line in 3 minutes or less | 100 |
| Cross the finish line in less than 2 minutes 45 seconds | 30 |
| Start on the green flag (lost on an early start) | 10 |
| End the race in the assigned pit box | 15 |
| Drop the red tire in the assigned pit box | 25 |
| Drop the red tire in an area other than the assigned pit box | 12 |
| Pick up a fresh blue tire from the assigned pit box | 40 |
| Pick up a fresh blue tire from an area other than the assigned pit box | 20 |
| No contact with the gray walls (per lap) | 15 |
| No contact with the vehicle obstacles (per lap) | 25 |
| Raise the school flag during the final lap | 25 |
| Team enters the publicity contest | 10 |

---

## Notes

### Finish Time
- 100 points for crossing the finish line in 3:00 or less.
- 30 more points for crossing in less than 2:45.
- V3.0 has no per-second speed bonus.

### Tire Exchange
- The robot starts with the red tire pre-loaded.
- Drop the red tire and pick up a blue tire during the pit stop.
- The blue tire must be a different tire than the one dropped.
- Drop and pickup score independently.
- The pickup points are removed if the robot drops the blue tire before it crosses the finish line.

### Clean Lap Bonuses
- Awarded per lap, not per race.
- The wall bonus and the obstacle bonus are separate.
- Maximum: 3 laps x (15 + 25) = 120 points.

### Early Start
- An early start removes the 10 point flag start bonus.
- It also gives a Yellow Card and a 2 point penalty (GM2 <G05>).

### "In" Rule
More than **75%** of the robot must be in the assigned pit box. No partial credit (GM2 3.3).
